The Margin logo and app icons in this folder: margin.svg (the master vector), icon.png (the
512 px runtime window icon), hicolor/ (Linux theme icons) and margin-macos-1024.png (the macOS
icon source). packaging/margin/icons.sh renders hicolor/ and margin-macos-1024.png from margin.svg.
